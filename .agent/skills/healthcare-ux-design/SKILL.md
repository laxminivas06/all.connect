# Healthcare UI/UX Design Skill

## Overview
Standards for building commercial-grade, human-centered healthcare SaaS interfaces across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## Design System Principles
1. **Clinical Trust & Clarity**:
   - Primary: Deep Medical Blue (`#0F4C81`, `slate-900`, `blue-700`) conveys stability and clinical authority.
   - Secondary: Clean Medical Teal (`#0D9488`, `teal-600`) represents care, primary medicine, and wellness.
   - Alert Accents: Amber for pharmacy/pending states, Emerald for confirmed/authorized states, Rose for cancellations and discounts.
   - Background: Soft Slate (`bg-slate-50`), crisp white cards (`bg-white border-slate-200/80`).
2. **Typography**:
   - Modern, readable sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter`).
   - Monospace formatting (`font-mono`) for all transaction IDs (`HC-2026-X`, `APT-2026-X`, `PRE-2026-X`, `PICK-XXXXX`).
3. **Role-Tailored Dashboards**:
   - **Patient**: Welcoming, cards-focused, large touch targets, accessible health ID card.
   - **Hospital / Clinic**: Data-dense, quick queue movements, revenue telemetry, action buttons.
   - **Doctor**: Distraction-free consultation room view, fast autocomplete-friendly prescription editor.
   - **Pharmacy**: High-contrast verification terminal for typing customer passcodes.
   - **Super Admin**: Executive KPI cards, toggle switches, and subscription revenue trends.
