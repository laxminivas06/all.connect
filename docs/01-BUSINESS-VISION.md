# 01: Business Vision & Commercial Model

## 1. Executive Vision
AllConnect aims to establish the digital operating system for urban healthcare delivery in India, beginning with Hyderabad. By bridging the gap between independent healthcare providers and patients, the platform unlocks network effects where every newly onboarded hospital or clinic increases the utility for all patients and pharmacies on the network.

## 2. Business Model: Dual-Sided Monetization
AllConnect employs a dual revenue model:

### A. B2B Healthcare SaaS Subscriptions
Hospitals and clinics pay recurring subscription fees to access platform management capabilities:
- **Starter Plan** (₹4,999/mo | ₹49,999/yr): For small independent clinics and polyclinics.
- **Professional Plan** (₹12,999/mo | ₹129,999/yr): For mid-sized multi-speciality medical centres.
- **Enterprise Plan** (₹29,999/mo | ₹299,999/yr): For tertiary hospital networks (Yashoda, Apollo, KIMS).

### B. B2C Healthcare Marketplace Ecosystem
- **Pharmacy Quotation Marketplace**: Pharmacies bid on patient prescriptions, driving price transparency and pharmacy footfall.
- **Promotional Campaign Placements**: Hospitals promote specialized health packages (e.g. Master Executive Health Checkup) directly to local patients.

## 3. The 3 Core Differentiators
1. **Single Centralized Identity**: Patient ID (`PAT-2026-X`) and Universal Health ID (`HC-2026-X`) recognized across all participating facilities.
2. **Reverse Pharmacy Quotation Engine**: Patients submit prescriptions; nearby registered pharmacies quote prices and delivery times in real time.
3. **Multi-Tenancy with Privacy**: Independent SaaS consoles for hospitals, with zero cross-tenant data leakage and granular patient consent controls.
